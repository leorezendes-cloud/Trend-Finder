# Trend Finder research views

The default `/trend-finder` view researches completed NFL games across teams, favorites, underdogs and totals. Each matchup is counted once. Separate home, away, favorite and underdog records prevent an artificial combined ATS percentage. A selected team gets its own record first. Pushes and ties are excluded from decision percentages; missing lines are reported separately.

Primetime Dogs remains available as a preset. The existing combination finder remains an underdog research tool. Existing saved searches without a view parameter retain their original underdog interpretation.

## Grouped filters

- 1 p.m. ET window: 13:00 through 13:59.
- 4 p.m. ET window: 16:00 through 16:59.
- Playoffs: WC, DIV, CON, SB and POST game types.
- Temperature: below 32°F, 32 to below 60°F, 60 to below 80°F, or 80°F and above.
- Wind: under 10 mph, 10 to below 20 mph, or 20 mph and above.

Exact recorded values remain available. Missing or nonfinite weather values do not match a weather band. These are historical recorded conditions, not guaranteed pregame forecasts. Rain/snow categories are unavailable in the current dataset. Roof can be selected separately. Score, result and overtime filters describe completed games and must not be interpreted as pregame evidence.

Results show 100 matchups per page. Searches can be saved with their selected team, role and conditions. This feature does not change the published prediction formula or locked picks.
