Add Trendfindah to your phone

Open scores, trends and The Plug straight from your home screen.

Free to install. Internet access is needed for current data and account features.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open trendfindah.com/install in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open Trendfindah in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install Trendfindah above if available, or open Chrome’s menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm installation and open Trendfindah from its icon.

Your existing account works in the app. You may need to sign in again the first time.
