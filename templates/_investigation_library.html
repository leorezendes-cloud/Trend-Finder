How we investigate

We check reporting, team accounts and game records before publishing. “Documented” describes the event, not every interpretation of it. Disputed details and gaps remain visible. A strange sequence is not evidence of game fixing.

Betting outcomes refer only to specifically documented lines. No assumed props, community tickets, or reconstructed prices are used. Cases are reviewed editorially; this is not a live news feed.
