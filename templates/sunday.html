📖 Understanding the Numbers

Watchlist Rank: Games ranked by the historical Wilson lower bound of their predefined spread category. Games with identical scores share the same rank.

Historical ATS: Percentage of underdogs that covered the spread in matching historical games. Pushes are excluded.

Historical Games: Number of games matching the filters.

Spread Range: The historical range containing the upcoming underdog's spread.

95% Lower Bound: The conservative end of our Wilson confidence interval. Higher values provide stronger historical evidence for a high ATS rate.

Important: These are historical patterns, not probabilities that an underdog will cover Sunday's game.
