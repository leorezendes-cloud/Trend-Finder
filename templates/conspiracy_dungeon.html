Mike's Fade the Public — Market Tallies

Mike's Rule: When at least 65% of public betting dollars back one side, take the opposite side. Reverse Line Movement (RLM) happens when recorded betting lines move against the side attracting most of the public money. It may suggest disagreement between betting activity and the market, but is not proof of sharp action or a guaranteed edge. Only documented qualifying signals are shown as selections.
