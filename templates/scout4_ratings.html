Experimental team strength and matchup estimates.

Scoring ratings use points; EPA measures play value above expectation. These are separate scales.

How ratings and estimates work

Ratings use all venues before the selected week. The statistical venue filter does not restrict these ratings; matchup venue is set separately above. Smaller samples shrink toward league average. Scoring strength is adjusted for opponents; recent form uses the last three games.

Passing and rushing ratings are in EPA per game relative to the league. Defensive ratings measure suppression; positive means stronger defense. Scoring offense and defense are in points per game above average. These scales are displayed separately.

Matchups below are hypothetical comparisons using today's historical data, not authenticated frozen pregame predictions. Injury and replacement effects are not included. No calibrated moneyline, ATS, or total probability is produced.
