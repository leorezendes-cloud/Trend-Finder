Original ATS and totals forecasts are locked before kickoff. They may use different lines and times from the public blend.

Methodology and experiment details

2026 shadow testing · first eligible pre-kickoff forecast stays frozen. These are experimental estimates, not validated confidence ratings. Pushes are graded separately. No injury, replacement-QB or weather adjustments enter these forecasts.

Totals use scoring, efficiency and play-volume proxies, not clock pace. Probabilities assume a continuous error distribution and do not estimate push likelihood.

Source timestamps, quoted odds and contribution breakdowns are preserved. Personnel/weather updates are separate evidence. Captures are maintained locally.
