About Gravy Train

Gravy Train brings together NFL trend research, locked predictions, team comparisons, and free member picks. Trendfindah 3000 is the NFL trend finder; The Plug publishes predictions; the Conspiracy Dungeon explores betting patterns and anomalies.

Where the information comes from

Schedules, historical results, and team/player statistics use saved nflverse data. The homepage scoreboard uses ESPN separately. Automatic final-score updates check matching nflverse and ESPN results. Market snapshots retain their sportsbook and observation time; the current quote collector uses DraftKings betting-split information.

Statistics and market quotes are saved snapshots. Their timestamps show their age. Missing information stays unavailable or pending.

Three kinds of lines

  • Market lines: the latest valid saved spreads and totals; these can change.
  • Member lines: a shared Wednesday 8 a.m. Eastern snapshot, fixed for everyone that week. Capture takes time, so actual observation and freezing times are recorded. The launch week uses its labelled one-time exception.
  • Plug lines: the original line saved with each pregame prediction. That line remains fixed for grading, even when the market moves.

How The Plug makes its picks

Plug’s Pick combines the Scout 4.0 scoring margin and the points-only scoring margin equally. Plug Total uses Scout’s separate total-points model. These estimates are experimental; an edge is not a calibrated probability or a guarantee.

The first eligible pregame prediction stays locked. New valid forecasts always select a side: an exact ATS tie uses the underdog, or the home team at pick’em; an exact total tie uses Under. Missing verified inputs still prevent a pick. Previously saved records keep their original policy.

The current blend does not add an injury, weather, or confirmed quarterback-replacement adjustment. Comparing completed performance describes past results; it does not establish what will happen next.

How records are graded

SU is the straight-up winner. ATS adds the chosen team’s saved spread to its final scoring margin. Over/Under compares the combined final score with the saved total. Equality is a push for ATS and totals; a tied final is a straight-up tie.

Member picks can be edited until the game’s locking deadline. Earlier schedule changes can move that deadline forward; postponing a game does not reopen locked picks. Verified final scores are required for grading. Canceled or unplayed games can be marked void with an explanation.

“ATS as Dog” means the team’s current-season regular-season cover–loss–push record as an underdog entering the selected week. “Result” means its ATS outcome in that matchup. Favorites, pick’em, unfinished games, and missing lines are excluded from the underdog record.

Reading trends and the Dungeon

Trend filters describe matching historical games. Always consider the sample size and time period; a small or selectively chosen sample can look stronger than it is. The Conspiracy Dungeon’s public-money patterns, referee assignments, line movement, and anomalies are research observations—not proof of manipulation or wrongdoing.
